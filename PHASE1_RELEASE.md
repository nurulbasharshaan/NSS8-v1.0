# Phase 1 Research Preview

This release is an architectural research snapshot of "1 MB 8 Bit Heterogeneous SoC with DMA and Dynamic Frequency Scaling for Low-Power IoT".

## Scope

- 8-bit CPU
- 20-bit physical addressing
- Lightweight DMA
- Fixed-priority CPU/DMA arbitration
- UART, SPI, Timer/PWM, GPIO
- Interrupt controller
- Software-defined clock division

## Important

This release does not claim complete RTL verification, timing closure,
post-layout power characterization, or silicon validation.

Known limitations are documented in `README.md`.
